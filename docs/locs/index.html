Loc Style Finder

38 men's loc styles, scored on 17 attributes

Every style is reduced to the same 17 attributes (thickness, length, how the sides are cut, how tidy the roots are, how it is worn, colour, upkeep, what the crown and the nape look like, and so on). Similarity is computed from those attributes only, never from a photo or the person wearing it. Open a style to see the closest matches and the furthest contrasts.

Head-to-head

Two or three deliberately different styles. Pick the one you would rather wear. Each pick sharpens your taste profile, and the next round is drawn partly from what you liked and partly from what you have not seen.

Compare side by side

Add two to four styles from Browse or from a style's page. Rows where the styles differ are highlighted.

Shortlist

Styles you have hearted or won in head-to-head rounds. Saved in this browser only.

Photos and prompts

The drawings are schematics. For realistic references, each style has a ready-made photo prompt built from the same attributes. Every prompt uses one fixed template (same man, same framing, same light, same backdrop) and only the hair sentences change, so the photos can be judged on the hairstyle alone. Each style has four prompts: front, side, back and top, because the crown and the nape can differ far more than the front does.

How similarity is scored

Each style is a point in a 17-attribute space. Distance between two styles is a weighted average of per-attribute differences: numeric attributes contribute how far apart they are on their scale, categorical ones contribute 0 (same) or 1 (different). Similarity is 1 minus that distance. Heavier weights go to the attributes a barber or loctician would name first: thickness, length and how the sides are cut.
